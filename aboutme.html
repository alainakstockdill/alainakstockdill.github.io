About Me

Last September, the Valdovinos Lab group spent the week at Bodega Bay Marine Labratory and we spent time exploring the intertidal organisms we study. Here is me with a sea star and a crab! When I'm not working on my research I love to spend time outside biking, running, and hiking. I spend my free evenings playing card games, cross-stitching, and reading.

I grew up in Spokane, Washington and decided to stay there for undergrad as well. I love my hometown but have loved getting to explore California during my time at Davis. My favorite things to do in the area are bike to Winters, visit San Francisco, and hike in Point Reyes. All of the photos included on my website are taken in some of my favorite places including Big Sur, Bodega Bay, and Point Reyes.
